WASM as multi-hop forwarder — native-A → WASM → native-B

WASM opens two vanilla channels (to A and B), seeds native-A's outbound liquidity, then native-A pays a BOLT11 invoice from native-B that it can only reach via the private-channel route hint (WASM→B). The HTLC is forwarded by the WASM node (autonomously, via the Phase 0.2 autoDrive loop) and settles end-to-end. Requires compose.wasm.yaml infra and two rgb-native-phase5-node instances (A on 19735/19737, B on 19745/19747). Driven headlessly by run_multihop_flow.mjs (auto-runs when ?phase= present).
